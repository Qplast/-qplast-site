Why Is the Body of a Cooking Oil Spray Matte?

The quality of cooking oil does not depend on the production stage alone; the type of packaging also plays a direct role in preserving the oil's flavour, colour and freshness after production. One of the important decisions in designing cooking oil spray packaging is the choice of body material and colour, a choice that may look purely cosmetic at first glance but has specific technical consequences.

Light, one of the main causes of oil quality loss

It is well known in food science that exposing cooking oils to direct light gradually speeds up oxidation and can affect the oil's flavour and colour. For this reason, oil producers also consider how much light reaches the product when choosing a packaging container.

Why a matte white or black metal body?

The body of Q Plast's cooking oil spray (Q Oil Spray) is metal, in matte white or black. Unlike clear bottles, which let light pass through easily, this metal body keeps ambient light away from the oil. This matters especially for brands that sell their product on store shelves exposed to artificial or natural light.

Metal body and PP pump with Food Grade resin

The body of Q Oil Spray is metal, and its pump is made of polypropylene (PP) with Food Grade resin. It should be noted that an official food-contact certificate has not currently been issued for this product, and Q Plast states this explicitly and unambiguously; the phrase "approved for food contact" is not correct for this product.

Q Oil Spray technical specifications

  • Neck size: 24
  • Height: 155 mm, diameter: 45 mm
  • Volume: 140 ml
  • Body: metal, matte white or black

The dimensions of this product are chosen to make kitchen use comfortable and also to suit arrangement on store shelves. To learn about the benefits of spray packaging itself in controlling oil use, read Cooking Oil Spray Packaging: A Solution for Controlled Use and Hygiene in Cooking.

Summary

Choosing the material and colour of the body in cooking oil spray packaging is a decision that goes beyond aesthetics and is directly linked to preserving product quality during storage.
