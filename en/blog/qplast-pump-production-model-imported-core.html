Why Is the Core of Q Plast Pumps Imported While the Other Parts Are Made in Iran?

When a cosmetic and personal-care brand goes looking for a pump supplier, there is usually one question in the back of its mind: where exactly is this part made? Q Plast answers that question transparently, because its production model follows a defined combination, not a simple "domestic" or "imported" label.

Q Plast's two-part production model

In Q Plast pumps, the core and chassis are imported. These two parts form the main mechanical part of the pump and are what guarantee the product's constant output. Alongside them, the other components of the pump are produced at Q Plast, and the final assembly of the product, from start to delivery, is also done inside the Q Plast factory.

This combination is the same in the Hamta, Arya, Pars and Arta groups. Sepanta follows the same logic, except that its mechanism is airless.

What does this model offer the buying brand?

When final assembly is carried out under Q Plast's direct supervision, quality control of the finished product also happens at that stage, not in a separate, untraceable step. This means that when a brand orders a new batch, the same assembly standard and the same production route are repeated.

In addition, producing the other parts inside Iran puts this part of the production chain directly in Q Plast's own hands.

Why does this transparency matter?

Q Plast presents this production model as part of its position: a technical partner that gives the buying brand the real specifications of the product, not just a parts supplier.

This approach is in line with Q Plast's mission: linking industrial engineering precision with the elegance of the cosmetics world, in a product whose specifications are clear from the start.

Summary

Q Plast's production model can be summed up as follows: imported core and chassis, with the remaining parts and final assembly in Iran. This combination is the basis of the consistent quality that cosmetic and personal-care brands need when choosing a pump supplier.
