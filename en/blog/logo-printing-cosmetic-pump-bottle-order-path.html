Does Q Plast Print Logos on Packaging Pumps (OEM)?

If your packaging needs logo printing or OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) services, it is best to know the answer to this question before requesting a quote from your pump supplier. It makes order planning and scheduling simpler.

Short answer: logo printing is not done in-house at Q Plast

Q Plast does not offer OEM services or logo printing on pumps or bottles in-house. This part of the process is referred to Q Plast's partner companies.

Two separate parts to plan

A brand that needs both a standard Q Plast component (Hamta, Aria, Pars, Arta or Sepanta) and logo printing should plan two parts separately. The first is sourcing components from Q Plast, following each group's component-sale rule (for example, Why Can't Every Cosmetic Pump Be Bought Separately from the Bottle?; and Sepanta is supplied only as a full pack, as explained in Sepanta Airless Pump: Why Do Air-Sensitive Formulas Need This Mechanism?). The second is logo printing through a partner that Q Plast introduces.

A note on delivery time

The delivery time Q Plast quotes is up to 10 working days for Hamta with white and black chassis and for the Aria, Pars and Arta groups (Why Has Delivery Time for Q Plast's White and Black Chassis Pumps Come Down to 10 Working Days?). For Hamta in a custom colour, delivery time is not fixed and the sales team quotes it separately; Sepanta delivery is 4 months.

These periods cover only preparing and handing over the component ex-works. Transport from the factory gate to the printing partner and the logo printing time are not included and must be planned separately. The printing schedule is set by the printing partner; Q Plast's sales team can introduce you to a partner company.

Questions to ask the printing partner before ordering

Before finalising the component order, ask the printing partner the following so that component quantity and print run are aligned:

  • The suitable printing method for the PET bottle body or the PP pump (or the dropper assembly in Arta)
  • The minimum print run
  • The printing time
  • How to collect the components from Q Plast's factory gate

For final compatibility, also consider testing samples before a bulk order (Why Do Professional Cosmetic Brands Test Pump Samples Before a Bulk Order?).

Summary

Brands that need logo printing should coordinate with Q Plast's sales team at the same time about the referral route to a printing partner. For a quote, contact Q Plast's sales team: 0990 970 2100.
