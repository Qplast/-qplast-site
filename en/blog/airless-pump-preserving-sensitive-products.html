Airless Pumps: A Way to Preserve the Quality of Sensitive Products

Cosmetic and personal-care products, especially those containing sensitive active ingredients, constantly face the challenge of preserving quality and efficacy over time. Oxidation and microbial contamination are among the main causes of reduced shelf life and performance. The airless pump, as an advanced packaging solution, offers an effective answer to this challenge and extends the useful life of sensitive formulations.

How airless pumps work: function and advantages

With its particular mechanism, the airless pump prevents air from entering the chamber. Unlike traditional pumps, which use a dip tube to draw up the product and let air into the bottle with every pump, airless pumps use a moving disc at the bottom of the bottle to push the product upward. This creates a vacuum and protects the chamber from contact with air. This engineered design provides high precision in preserving the integrity of the formulation.

Benefits of airless pumps for sensitive formulations

Formulations containing antioxidants, vitamins (such as vitamins C and E) and peptides urgently need protection because of their high sensitivity to air. Contact of these ingredients with oxygen can lead to oxidation, colour change, reduced efficacy and even spoilage. By eliminating contact between the product and air, an airless pump protects these active ingredients from degradation and ensures their shelf life and efficacy throughout the period of use. This feature is vital for products with high performance claims.

Airless versus traditional pumps: effect on shelf life and efficacy

The main difference between airless and traditional pumps is how the product is dispensed. Traditional pumps let some air into the chamber with each use, which gradually causes oxidation and reduces product quality. By contrast, airless pumps keep the product fresh to the last drop by maintaining a vacuum environment and preventing air from entering. This not only extends shelf life but also ensures that the consumer always enjoys a product with its original quality and efficacy. Studies have shown that products packaged with an airless pump can have up to 15% longer shelf life than similar samples with traditional pumps.

Key points in choosing the right airless pump

Choosing the right airless pump requires attention to several key factors to ensure full compatibility with the product formulation:

  • Pump material: it must be compatible with the product formulation so that no undesirable chemical reaction occurs. The polymer materials used in airless pumps must have the required chemical resistance to the active ingredients, pH and solvents in the formulation.
  • Pump capacity: it should be designed to suit the product volume and frequency of use. Choosing the right capacity both prevents product waste and provides an optimal user experience.
  • Viscosity compatibility: the chosen pump must match the product's physical properties (such as viscosity) so that it works smoothly and consistently.
