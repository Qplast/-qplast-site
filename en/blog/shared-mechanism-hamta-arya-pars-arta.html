One Mechanism, Four Neck Sizes: Why Do Hamta, Arya, Pars and Arta Share a Common Base?

At first glance, the Hamta, Arya, Pars and Arta groups look like four different products. In practice, all of them use a similar pump mechanism. The only exception among Q Plast's products is the Sepanta group, with its airless mechanism.

What a shared mechanism means in practice

The pump core and chassis in all these groups are imported, the other parts are produced at Q Plast, and final assembly is also done by Q Plast. This means the pump behaves the same way when pressed in the Hamta, Arya, Pars and Arta groups; the real difference between these groups is only in the neck size and the compatible bottle volume.

The groups differ only in neck size and volume

Hamta is designed with a 24 neck for volumes of 90, 100, 140, 170 and 220 ml. Arya with an 18 neck and Pars with a 20 neck are both suitable for volumes of 30, 50 and 80 ml. Arta, made for dropper use, comes in two neck sizes: its 18 neck is like Arya and its 20 neck is like Pars.

A fixed 1.3 ml output in all groups

In all four groups, Hamta, Arya, Pars and Arta, the output per press of the pump is 1.3 ml. This figure stays constant across all groups and does not change with the chosen neck size or bottle volume.

What advantage does this uniformity give brands?

For a brand with several products in different volumes, this uniform mechanism means the user experience does not change between products; only the neck size and bottle volume are chosen according to the formula and target packaging. The pump body in all these groups is PP and the bottle body is PET; both are Food Grade resins.

Sepanta, the airless exception

The Sepanta group is the only one that does not follow this shared mechanism and is offered as airless. This group is available only as a full pack, with a 4-month delivery time. To choose the right group, fill in the inquiry form.
