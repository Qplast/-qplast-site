Pump and Bottle Body Materials in Q Plast Products: PP and PET

In industrial beauty and personal-care packaging, knowing exactly what each component is made of matters to brands before they order. This article reviews the pump and bottle body materials for each Q Plast product group.

Hamta, Aria and Pars: PP pump body, PET bottle body

In the Hamta, Aria and Pars groups, the pump body is made of polypropylene (PP) and the bottle body is polyethylene terephthalate (PET). These three groups use a similar pump mechanism (One Mechanism, Four Neck Sizes: Why Do Hamta, Arya, Pars and Arta Share a Common Base?).

Arta: no pump, but the bottle is still PET

The Arta group has no pump and uses a dropper assembly instead. Like Hamta, Aria and Pars, the Arta bottle is made of PET.

Sepanta: an airless pump made of PP

The Sepanta group has an airless pump, also made of PP (Sepanta Airless Pump: Why Do Air-Sensitive Formulas Need This Mechanism?). For the neck and bottle specifications of Sepanta, contact Q Plast's sales team.

Food Grade resin is not a food-contact certificate

The resin used to make Q Plast pumps (PP) and bottles (PET) is Food Grade for all production. This refers only to the type of raw material and does not mean the finished product is certified for direct food contact.

Summary for brands

Knowing exactly what each component is made of in each group — PP for the pump bodies of Hamta, Aria and Pars and the Sepanta airless pump, and PET for the bottle bodies of Hamta, Aria, Pars and Arta — helps brands plan their orders. Final compatibility with each formulation should be checked by testing samples before a bulk order (Why Do Professional Cosmetic Brands Test Pump Samples Before a Bulk Order?).
