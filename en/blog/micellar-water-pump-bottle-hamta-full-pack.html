Micellar Water Pump and Bottle: Why Is the Hamta Full Pack a Good Choice?

Why micellar water needs a precise pump

Micellar water and toner are products that consumers use every day, several times a day. For that reason, the pump that delivers these products to the pad or hand must dispense the same amount on every press. Fluctuation in pump output makes the usage experience inconsistent for the end customer and affects their perception of the brand's quality.

The Hamta micellar water full pack: a clear option for this category

The Hamta group, with a 24 neck and a constant output of 1.3 ml, is available in volumes of 90, 100, 140, 170 and 220 ml. The pump body is PP and the bottle body is PET, and the resin of both is Food Grade.

According to Q Plast's official price list, the empty Hamta pump for micellar water costs 57,000 Toman (white chassis), 58,000 Toman (black) and 61,000 Toman (coloured). The full pack (pump + bottle) equals the sum of the pump and bottle prices: 75,000 Toman with a white chassis, 76,000 Toman with a black chassis and 80,000 Toman with a coloured chassis and bottle.

The empty Hamta bottle is currently not sold on its own; this group can be bought only as an empty pump or a full pack (pump + bottle).

Delivery time and minimum order

Under Q Plast's fixed rule, orders for Hamta packs with a white or black chassis are delivered within a maximum of 10 working days. For coloured or custom chassis, delivery time is not announced directly and must be arranged with the sales team.

The minimum order (MOQ) for the Hamta group is one carton, equal to 750 pieces, a figure that new brands in skin care can also plan around.

Summary

For brands that make micellar water, toner or skin-cleansing solutions, the Hamta micellar water full pack is a clear option with a clear price and delivery time. Delivery is on Ex-Works (at the factory gate) terms and shipping costs are borne by the customer.
