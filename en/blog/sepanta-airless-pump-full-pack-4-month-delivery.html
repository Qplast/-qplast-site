Sepanta Airless Pump: Full Pack Only and 4-Month Delivery — What to Know Before You Order

Brands with air-sensitive formulas often look at airless pumps. In the Q Plast product family, Sepanta is the only group that uses an airless mechanism. Before you order, however, there are two practical points about how Sepanta is sold and delivered that should be built into your brand planning.

What is Sepanta?

Unlike Hamta, Arya and Pars, which share a similar pump mechanism, Sepanta works on an airless principle. Its compatible volumes are 30, 50 and 80 ml, and like Hamta, Arya and Pars, its pump body is PP. For the neck size and other technical details of Sepanta, and to check its compatibility with your formula, please coordinate with the Q Plast sales team.

Full pack only; no single components

Component sales rules differ across Q Plast product groups (see the full explanation in our article Why Can't Every Cosmetic Pump Be Bought Separately from the Bottle?). Sepanta has its own rule: unlike groups that allow partial purchases, Sepanta is supplied only as a complete pack, and the pump or the bottle cannot be bought on its own.

4-month delivery, with no faster option

Sepanta's delivery time is 4 months. This figure applies to all orders in this group, and at present no faster delivery option is offered. It is considerably longer than the delivery time for Hamta, Arya, Pars and Arta with white or black bodies, which is up to 10 working days. For brands with production or launch schedules, factoring in these 4 months is essential. For example, if a product launch is planned for a specific date, the Sepanta order must be placed at least 4 months before it, plus shipping and filling-line time at the brand's facility.

Full pack price

The full pack price for Sepanta with 4-month delivery is USD 0.801 for white or black bodies and USD 0.825 for coloured bodies. Delivery is Ex-Works (EXW), and freight is the customer's responsibility. (Prices are based on Q Plast's official price list valid at the time of publication, November 2026.)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Sepanta be delivered faster?

No. Currently no faster delivery option is offered for Sepanta, and its delivery time is fixed at 4 months.

Can I buy only the pump or only the bottle of Sepanta?

No. Sepanta is supplied only as a complete pack.

Who pays the shipping for Sepanta?

Delivery is Ex-Works (EXW), which means shipping costs after pickup at the factory are paid by the customer.

Conclusion

If you need an airless pump, Sepanta is Q Plast's option, provided your brand can order a complete pack and fit a 4-month delivery time into its schedule. Confirm both points with the sales team before placing an order.
