A Guide to Choosing the Order Volume (MOQ) of Packaging Pumps for Start-Up Cosmetic and Personal-Care Brands

Every packaging pump manufacturer defines a minimum order quantity (MOQ) for each product group. This figure directly affects a brand's working capital and warehouse volume, especially for new brands that do not yet have a stable sales pattern.

Why does MOQ matter in cosmetic and personal-care packaging?

At Q Plast the minimum order for each group is one carton, and the quantity per carton differs by group. Ordering more than you really need locks capital in the warehouse; ordering below the MOQ is simply not possible. That is why knowing each group's MOQ precisely, before the final choice, is a serious part of purchasing planning.

Comparison of MOQ across Q Plast pump groups

  • Hamta (24 mm neck, volumes 90 to 220 ml): 1 carton = 750 pieces, the lowest minimum order among Q Plast groups
  • Arya (18 neck): 1 carton = 1,500 pieces
  • Pars (20 neck): 1 carton = 1,500 pieces
  • Arta (dropper, 18 or 20 neck): 1 carton = 1,500 pieces
  • Sepanta (airless group): minimum order is set by inquiry with the sales team

How to match order volume to your sales plan

A brand launching its first product without enough sales data usually starts with a group that has a lower MOQ to reduce the risk of excess stock. A brand that has already sold the same product and has a clear sales pattern can choose higher MOQs with more confidence.

Comparing each group's MOQ against the sales forecast for the next 3 to 6 months is a more practical yardstick than deciding on unit price alone.

Delivery time and its link to your order choice

For the Hamta group, white and black chassis are always in stock and standard delivery is up to 10 working days; delivery times for custom colours are announced by the Q Plast sales team. Arya, Pars and Arta are also delivered in up to 10 working days in the standard case.

These time frames, along with MOQ, should be factored into the brand's launch or restocking schedule.

Summary

Choosing the right MOQ is a balance between available cash, warehouse space and a realistic sales estimate. With a one-carton minimum order in the Hamta, Arya, Pars and Arta groups, Q Plast lets each brand choose according to its growth stage.
