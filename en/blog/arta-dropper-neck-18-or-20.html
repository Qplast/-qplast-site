Arta Dropper: Neck 18 or 20, Which Matches Arya or Pars?

Brands that use a serum with a dropper and also another product with an Arya or Pars pump usually look for visual consistency between their bottles. In the Q Plast product family, the Arta bottle is shared with the Arya and Pars bottles.

What is Arta?

Arta has no pump. It uses a dropper assembly instead, and its output is measured in drops rather than a fixed millilitre figure. Compatible volumes for Arta, like Arya and Pars, are 30, 50 and 80 ml.

Neck 18 like Arya, neck 20 like Pars

Arta is offered in two neck sizes. Arta with neck 18 uses the same bottle as Arya, and Arta with neck 20 uses the same bottle as Pars. This means that if a brand already has a product with an Arya or Pars bottle on its line, it can add a matching Arta dropper using the same bottle. The only difference is the closure head: a dropper instead of a pump.

Why this matching matters

When several products from one brand sit side by side on a shelf or in a customer's hand, a shared bottle keeps the product line visually unified, without the need to design a new bottle.

Material and delivery

The Arta bottle is made of PET, and its resin is Food Grade. The minimum order quantity (MOQ) for both Arta neck sizes is one carton of 1,500 units. Delivery for Arta with white or black bodies, like Arya and Pars, is up to 10 working days. For coloured bodies, please ask the sales team about delivery time.

Frequently Asked Questions

If my brand has no product with an Arya or Pars bottle, which neck should I choose?

Both Arta neck sizes come in the same volumes. In that case, choose the neck based on the pump family you are likely to add later (18 for Arya, 20 for Pars), or consult the Q Plast sales team.

Can I buy only the Arta dropper without the bottle?

No. Under Q Plast's component sales rules, the dropper is not sold on its own. The bottle and cap pack can be purchased.

Is Arta available in coloured bodies?

Yes. Arta is available in white or black and in coloured bodies. The price difference between the two is set out in the price section of this article.

Conclusion

To choose the right Arta neck, simply look at the bottle of the product family you already use: if you use Arya, choose neck 18; if you use Pars, choose neck 20. For the current daily dropper price and to request a sample, contact the Q Plast sales team at +98 990 970 2100.
