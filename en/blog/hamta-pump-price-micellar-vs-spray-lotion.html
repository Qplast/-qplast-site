Hamta Pump Prices for Spray, Lotion and Micellar Water: What to Specify in Your Inquiry

A common question when requesting a quote

When beauty and personal-care brands request a quote for the Hamta pump, they sometimes see two different figures for what looks like the same pump. The reason is that Q Plast's price list records the Hamta pump in two separate rows, based on the formulation of the finished product: one row for spray and lotion, and one for micellar water.

Two price rows for the empty Hamta pump

FormulationWhiteBlackColoured
Spray and lotion50,000 toman51,000 toman53,000 toman
Micellar water57,000 toman58,000 toman61,000 toman

In all three colours, the micellar water pump is priced 7,000 to 8,000 toman higher than the spray and lotion pump, while the overall Hamta pump mechanism is the same in both cases (the same mechanism described in One Mechanism, Four Neck Sizes: Why Do Hamta, Arya, Pars and Arta Share a Common Base?). Technical details of each version are available from Q Plast's sales team.

The price difference by chassis colour (white, black or coloured) is a separate topic, explained in Why Do Coloured Pumps and Bottles Cost More Than White and Black?.

Why specify this in your inquiry?

If you only say "Hamta pump" when requesting a price, Q Plast's sales team will need to ask about the formulation of your finished product to give an exact price. Stating whether the product is a spray, lotion or micellar water helps you get the right price at the first step.

This also matters for the full-pack price (pump plus bottle): the Hamta bottle is not sold separately and is supplied only as part of the full pack. For example, a Hamta full pack with a white spray pump and a clear bottle comes to 68,000 toman (50,000 + 18,000), while the same colour combination with a micellar water pump comes to 75,000 toman (57,000 + 18,000). For more details, see Micellar Water Pump and Bottle: Why Is the Hamta Full Pack a Good Choice?.

The formulation does not affect delivery time; the 10-day delivery rule for white and black chassis is covered in Why Has Delivery Time for Q Plast's White and Black Chassis Pumps Come Down to 10 Working Days?.

Summary

In Q Plast's price list, the Hamta pump has separate rows for spray and lotion and for micellar water. Stating the exact formulation at the start of your inquiry is the simplest way to get the correct price. For a quote, call Q Plast's sales team on +98 990 970 2100.

The price difference is not only about formulation; colour also plays a major role in the final cost. Every time production changes colour, some material is scrapped as waste before the desired shade is reached, and the same happens again when switching back to the previous colour. In addition, the cost price of the coloured material itself is considerably higher. For these reasons, a coloured pump is priced higher than white and black.
