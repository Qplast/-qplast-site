Cooking Oil Spray for Air Fryers: What Pump Packaging Offers Oil Brands

Cooking oil spray for air fryers answers a simple need: an air fryer usually works with a small amount of oil, and that small amount is sprayed rather than poured. For this reason, spray packaging has become an option worth considering for cooking oil producers.

Signals from the global market

In a report dated 23 August 2024, FoodNavigator-USA stated that the Bertolli brand (owned by Deoleo) had launched an olive oil spray called "Bertolli Olive Oil for Air Fryers". According to the same report, the product is a non-aerosol spray without propellants, and its pack carries the callout "suitable for high temperature". That callout concerns Bertolli's oil and is not a claim about Q Plast packaging.

According to a headline in The Grocer, the British brand Frylight has also launched a pure oil spray made for air fryers. According to a Food & Drink Network report dated 14 May 2026, the brand's new all-plastic, recyclable spray pump began its rollout in May 2026. In both examples, the spray packaging format is highlighted in the product's presentation.

The role of the pump in spray packaging

A spray pump is mechanical and works with hand pressure. For an oil brand, choosing the right pump and neck starts at the very stage of deciding on the packaging. The oil is sprayed onto the food before it is placed in the appliance.
