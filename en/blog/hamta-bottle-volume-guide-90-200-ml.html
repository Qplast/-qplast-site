Hamta Bottle Size Guide: 90 to 200 ml

Brands looking for a pump with a shared neck and several bottle sizes usually evaluate the Hamta series. Q Plast offers this group in five volumes. This guide shows those volumes and explains how to choose between them.

What Hamta is

Hamta is a group of Q Plast pumps for cosmetic and personal care packaging (spray, lotion and micellar water). It fits a 24 neck. The pump mechanism is similar to the Arya and Pars groups. The pump body is PP, and the bottle (in a complete pack) is PET.

Compatible volumes: five options from 90 to 200 ml

20024PET

The largest Hamta bottle is 200 ml. Larger volumes are not available for this group. All five volumes share a 24 neck, and the Hamta pump fits every one of them.

How to choose a bottle volume

Decide first whether your product launches in a travel or trial size (90 and 100 ml) or a longer-use size (140 to 200 ml). Then decide whether you plan to offer one formula in several volumes. Before a bulk order, request a sample to compare the volumes in practice.

Sales, MOQ and delivery

An empty Hamta bottle is not sold on its own. It is sold only as a complete pack (pump + bottle). The pump can be bought separately (details in the article “Why Can't Every Cosmetic Pump Be Bought Separately from the Bottle?”). The MOQ is one carton of 750 pieces. With a white or black chassis, delivery takes up to 10 working days. Delivery is Ex-Works, and the customer covers freight. For custom or special colours, no public delivery time is published; please coordinate with the sales team.

Prices

Hamta bottle price (complete pack only): clear USD 0.069, coloured USD 0.073. The pump price starts at USD 0.408 for a white spray or lotion pump and varies by chassis colour and formulation (spray/lotion or micellar water); details are in the article “Hamta Pump Prices for Spray, Lotion and Micellar Water: What to Specify in Your Inquiry”. A complete pack costs the sum of the pump and the bottle. For example, a white spray pump with a clear bottle costs USD 0.477. For the exact price of each volume, contact the sales team at +98 990 970 2100.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hamta available in 220 ml?

No. The largest available Hamta volume is 200 ml.

Can I buy only the Hamta bottle, without a pump?

No. An empty Hamta bottle is not sold alone; it is available only as a complete pack.

What is the delivery time for Hamta in a custom or special colour?

A public delivery time is not published. Please contact the sales team at +98 990 970 2100.

Conclusion

To choose the right Hamta volume from the five options between 90 and 200 ml, define your target usage size and your chassis colour. The neck is 24 in all five volumes. To request a quote or a sample, contact the Q Plast sales team at +98 990 970 2100.
