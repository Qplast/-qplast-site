Cooking Oil Spray Packaging: A Solution for Controlled Use and Hygiene in Cooking

Today's consumers want tighter control over the oil they use. Spray packaging spreads a thin, even layer of oil, which makes controlling consumption easier and reduces direct contact between the environment, hands and the product.

Q Oil Spray technical specifications

  • Neck size: 24
  • Bottle volume: 140 ml
  • Height: 155 mm, diameter: 45 mm
  • Pump body: polypropylene (PP), matte white or black
  • Bottle body: polyethylene terephthalate (PET)
  • Pump and bottle raw material: Food Grade resin

An important note on direct food contact

The raw material of Q Oil Spray is Food Grade, but an official certificate for direct food contact of the finished product has not yet been issued and is under technical review. We state this openly so industrial customers can make precise decisions.
